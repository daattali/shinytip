# shinytip (development version)

- New `tip_update()` function for changing the text of a tooltip from the server. Give the tooltip a
`tip_id` when creating it with `tip()`, `tip_icon()`, or `tip_input()`. Both `content` and
`content_disabled` can be changed, added, or removed.
